Contents (extracted to data/cifar-10-batches-py/ on first run by src/data.py):
  data_batch_1 .. data_batch_5   50,000 training images, 5,000 per class
  test_batch                     10,000 test images,     1,000 per class
  batches.meta                   the ten class names

Images: 32 x 32 RGB, uint8, no missing values.
Classes: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck.

Integrity check (asserted in src/data.py on every run): training array shape
(50000, 32, 32, 3) and dtype uint8 with values spanning 0-255; exactly 5,000
training and 1,000 test images per class; and per-channel training means/standard
deviations of (0.4914, 0.4822, 0.4465) / (0.2470, 0.2435, 0.2616), recomputed from
the raw pixels rather than trusted from the configuration file. The archive's MD5
is verified against Krizhevsky's published checksum before extraction.

Partitioning: the official 10,000-image test split is held out untouched and scored
once per model. A stratified 5,000-image validation split (500 per class) is drawn
from the 50,000 official training images under master seed 20260726, leaving 45,000
for training. Model selection uses validation accuracy only.
